## Palette 1: Tech Content (High-Energy Disruptor)
Order of use: Base/Box -> Accents -> Text

* #03045E (Deep Blue): Use for 60% of the slide (background overlays/solid text boxes).
* #E63946 (Normal Red): Use for 30% of the slide (subheadings/box structures).
* #FF5400 (Hot Orange): Use for 10% of the slide (critical keywords/call-to-action).
* #FF0055 (Hot Red): Alternative accent for urgent headlines.
* #FF9E00 (Clear Orange): Alternative text highlight for high readability inside dark boxes.

## Palette 2: Business Content (Editorial Intellectual)
Order of use: Base/Box -> Mid-Tones -> Text

* #0B3C49 (Deep Forest Teal): Use for 60% of the slide (photo filters/heavy base graphics).
* #1A2F25 (Deep Sage Green): Alternative dark base/box color for a premium academic look.
* #C87A53 (Muted Terracotta): Use for 30% of the slide (mid-tone floating cards/frames).
* #EED9C4 (Soft Sand Cream): Use for paragraphs and body text inside dark elements.
* #CCFF00 (Acid Lime): Use for 10% of the slide (strictly for headline hooks and icons).

## Palette 3: Neutral Posts (Minimalist)
Order of use: Floating Box -> Text/Shadow -> Background Overlay

* #E2E8F0 (Sophisticated Slate): Use for the solid floating shadow box card.
* #03045E (Deep Blue): Use for typography inside the slate box and for the faint outer drop shadow.
* Desaturated Photo Background: Use raw lifestyle photos with reduced color saturation beneath the box.
